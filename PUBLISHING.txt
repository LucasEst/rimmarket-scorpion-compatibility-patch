WORKSHOP PUBLISHING - VERSION 1.0.0

This mod folder is prepared for uploading through RimWorld's Workshop
publishing interface. It has not been published automatically.

1. First test in the save that reproduces the error: remove Scorpions from
   stalls, cancel their sale orders, enable the patch and restart the game.
   Check the art bench's right-click menu and confirm that a Scorpion cannot
   be restocked. Also check that an ordinary item can still be sold.
2. Optionally replace the author field in About/About.xml with your username.
3. Publish the patch folder as a local mod from the game.
   About/Preview.png supplies the thumbnail; WORKSHOP_DESCRIPTION.txt contains
   the English description to copy onto the Workshop page.
4. Add these three required items to the Steam Workshop page:
   Harmony: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077
   RimMarket: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3817820660
   VFE Classical: https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2787850474
5. Keep the testing limitations in the initial description. After testing
   in the affected save, update that section with the actual results.

Do not reuse another mod's PublishedFileId. Steam will assign one during the
first publication. Keep the ID assigned to THIS patch for future updates.

Original RimWorld, RimMarket, VFE Classical and Harmony DLLs are not included.
Only the patch's own code is distributed, under the MIT license.
